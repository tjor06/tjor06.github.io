01 About

I'm an undergraduate at UC Berkeley (Class of 2028) studying Physics with a minor in Statistics, raised in New York City with an Icelandic heritage. My coursework currently spans from multivariable calculus, linear algebra, and differential equations through mechanics, special relativity, electricity & magnetism, and introductory quantum mechanics and thermodynamics.

Professionally, I like to work where technical problems need to be engineered around. This summer I worked as an AI Infrastructure Engineering Intern with the United Nations (contractor via AI Learning & Digital Safety), where I architected and shipped a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. Using a hexagonal architecture with configuration-driven composition, it had isolation user-session capabilites, and automatic credential redaction. This culminated in a full technical document generated to assist the engineers continue building MCP capabilities. At UC Berkeley, I served as President of Phi Kappa Psi, managing a $120,000+ annual budget, executing a debt-turnaround plan that eliminated $16,000 in debt, and raising $10,000 for charity.

Outside of class I enjoy poker, piano performance and composition, playing basketball, writing and reading in English, French, or Icelandic.
